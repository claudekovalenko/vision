Where do I want to raise a family, and with whom?

A deep-down question. Thinking about my kids: where they can be raised up, and who they grow up around.

The place question waits on the church question. I don’t feel settled on where to live mostly because I don’t yet have a solid church community where I’d be happy to see my children grow. Find the people first; the place tends to follow.

How they’re raised

Homeschool, and the kids they grow up around

  • Pretty set on homeschooling, maybe with some online classes.
  • Around other kids I’d be happy for mine to be like and be influenced by.
  • Not outsourcing it all: the home comes first.

How do I get kids that look like Leo’s kids? Is it the kids around them, or the discipline and discipleship at home? Probably both, in that order: what children see in their parents and home forms them most, and good friends reinforce it. The best way to find out is to ask Leo’s parents what they did.

Weighing it, in order

Where to live

  • 1. A church community where I’d be glad to see my children grow. The only place I’ve seen it done well: the church in Hawaii.
  • 2. Families whose kids I’d want mine to be like.
  • 3. Missional fit: college ministry, campuses within reach.
  • 4. What I love: Orange County, next to Disneyland and Knott’s Berry Farm. Practical and a little selfish, and that’s okay to name.
  • 5. The hard parts: Southern California is not easy: cost, and how far left the culture is.
